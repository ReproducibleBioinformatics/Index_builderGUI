Add a tool / version

A tool needs three things: a Dockerfile, an index command, and (optionally) parameters. Folders are created automatically and the tool becomes selectable for indexing.

Placeholders

{fasta} and {gtf} are filled in by the service: once the user picks an organism and release, the real Ensembl files are resolved, downloaded and decompressed, and their paths are substituted here. You never declare them. Parameters are only for tunables.

{fasta}Genome FASTA (downloaded automatically)
{gtf}Annotation GTF (downloaded automatically)
{index_dir}Output folder (created for you)
{threads}CPU threads
{sjdb_overhang}STAR sjdbOverhang
{sa_index_nbases}STAR genomeSAindexNbases (auto from genome size)
{genome_length}Total genome length
{name}Any parameter you declare above

Anything you declare in Parameters shows up as an editable field in the index form, pre-filled with its default.
